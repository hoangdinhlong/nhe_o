## 7. Round 3 Critical Findings (`R3-CRIT-01` .. `R3-CRIT-03`)

| ID | Subsystem | Detailed Finding & Failure Mechanism | Remediation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| `R3-CRIT-01` | `Nhe_O.Transaction` / `Nhe_O.RAG` / `Nhe_O.C_API` | **CLOG Horizon Cliff at `Tx_Id > 1024` (`Max_CLOG_Entries = 1024` vs. `Max_Tx_Id = 1_000_000_000`) & Shared `G_Next_Tx` Depletion via Durable KV (`src/nhe_o-transaction.ads:20-71`, `src/nhe_o-transaction.adb:17-26, 424-441`, `src/nhe_o-c_api.adb:413-668`):** (1) `Set_CLOG_State` silently ignores `Id > Max_CLOG_Entries (1024)` and `Is_Tx_Committed` unconditionally returns `False` for `Id > 1024`. Consequently, any document ingested or updated at `Tx_Id > 1024` is permanently invisible (`Is_Tuple_Visible = False`); any document committed at `Xmin <= 1024` and deleted at `Xmax > 1024` evaluates `not Is_Tx_Committed (CLOG, Header.Xmax) = True` and resurrects as an undeletable zombie; and `Redo_WAL_Recovery` discards all committed transactions with `Tx_Id > 1024`. (2) In `Nhe_O.C_API`, `C_KV_Set`, `C_KV_Del`, `C_KV_Expire`, and `C_KV_Incr_By` increment the shared global `G_Next_Tx`, so 1,024 durable KV operations exhaust the CLOG window even with zero RAG transactions. | ☐ Scheduled in Epic 04 (Task 12) |
| `R3-CRIT-02` | `Nhe_O.Transaction` / `Nhe_O.RAG` / `Nhe_O.C_API` | **Silent WAL Truncation at 256 Frames (`Max_Commit_Scan = 256`) & Silent Skipping of Corrupted `WAL_Frame_Data` Frames in `Commit_Tx` (`src/nhe_o-transaction.adb:258, 288-310`, `src/nhe_o-rag.adb:396`, `src/nhe_o-c_api.adb:183-198`):** (1) `Commit_Tx` scans only WAL frames `0 .. 255` (`Max_Commit_Scan = 256`) without checking `Scan_EOF` and without checkpointing/truncating `.wal`. Once `.wal` contains `>= 256` frames, `Commit_Tx` appends the commit marker at index `>= 256`, scans only `0 .. 255`, never materializes the new `WAL_Frame_Data` (`"TOAS"`/`"VTST"`) pages to `DB_Path`, writes Page 1 (`"SLOT"`), and returns `Status_Ok` — causing subsequent `Execute_Hybrid_RAG_Query` calls to fail with `Status_IO_Error`. (2) At `src/nhe_o-transaction.adb:297`, `Commit_Tx` silently ignores `WAL_Frame_Data` frames that fail `Verify_WAL_Frame` (CRC32 mismatch) instead of aborting. (3) `Recover_RAG_Engine` and `C_Init_With_Paths` also hardcode `256` frames (`0 .. 255`). | ☐ Scheduled in Epic 04 (Task 12) |
